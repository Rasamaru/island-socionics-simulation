# Data

Planned structured datasets:

- `participants.json` — the 64 agents and their individual attributes.
- `settlements.json` — four starting settlements and regional conditions.
- `resources.csv` — resource observations by settlement and simulation day.
- `relationships.csv` — observed social ties, with evidence and timestamps.

Keep stable identifiers separate from display names. Preserve the distinction between known starting attributes and later observations. Do not invent missing values; use null or an explicit unknown marker.
